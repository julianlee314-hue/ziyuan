前言

台灣華語字圃 · Traditional · 注音優先 · 不是 HSK

字園把每個漢字當成一株植物。 苗圃裡是種子；播進園裡之後，要靠澆水——間隔提取——才會長。 重讀不是澆水。

這一圃的約定：

  • 只用繁體，語感貼台灣華語。
  • 注音在前；拼音可開，但不當主軸。
  • 不是依 HSK 分級。一圃叫市井：路上、螢幕、對話裡會撞到的字。

Plot one = 市井 1200。 目錄已上架 1200 個繁體單字，注音（含破音）齊備。 帳本（ledger）欄位仍在 schema 裡；本階段多數字為精簡預設，不做逐字創作 hook。 二～五圃尚空。

園裡的 SRS、筆記、自訂 mem 只存在你的瀏覽器。 清空本機園不會動到目錄。

Zìyuán treats every Chinese character as a plant. The nursery holds seeds; once planted in the garden, they grow through watering—spaced retrieval—not re-reading.

This plot’s rules:

  • Traditional characters only, tuned to Taiwan Mandarin.
  • Zhuyin first; pinyin is optional, never the spine.
  • Not HSK-graded. Plot one is Street: characters you meet on the road, on screens, in talk.

Plot one = Street 1200. The catalog now ships 1200 Traditional singles with exhaustive Zhuyin (including alternate readings). Ledger fields remain in the schema; this pass uses minimal defaults rather than hand-authored hooks. Plots 2–5 are still empty.

Garden SRS, notes, and custom mems live only in your browser. Clearing the local garden does not touch the catalog.
